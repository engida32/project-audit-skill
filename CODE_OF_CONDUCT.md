# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Critique ideas, code, and evidence, not
people. Keep discussion focused on making the skill safer and more useful.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Posting secrets, private repository data, or exploit details in public.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or reviews.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and any other space
where someone represents this project.

## Reporting

Report a concern to the maintainer, Engida Lishan, at
[engida32lishan@gmail.com](mailto:engida32lishan@gmail.com). Reports are handled
privately. The maintainer may edit or remove content, close a thread, or block
an account.

## Enforcement

Responses scale with the behavior: a private warning, a temporary block, or a
permanent ban. The maintainer decides, and the decision is final for this
project.
